## Privacy policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026.

This policy explains what data News App handles and why, in the same plain language we ask article authors to write in.

### Who we are

News App is an app where any registered person can publish their own articles. We, the TrikeDevs team, operate it and are responsible for your data.

### What we collect

**When you create an account** (email and password, or Google):

- Email address — stays with the authentication provider, never stored in the articles database.
- Display name and profile photo (optional) — these are public, since they appear next to what you publish.

**When you publish an article**: title, body, category, cover image, and your name/photo become visible to anyone using the app. Drafts are visible only to you.

**When you report an article**: we store the reason, an optional note, and who reported it. The author of the reported article never sees who reported them — that information is for the review team only.

**On your phone**: we store your preferences (theme, language, accessible mode) and a local copy of the articles you marked "Read it later", so they open faster and stay available offline.

### What we don't collect

We don't use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. We don't sell or share your data with anyone outside the services that run the app.

### Who we share data with

- **Google Firebase** (authentication, database, and image storage) — hosts your account and your articles.
- **Google Sign-In** — only if you choose to sign in with your Google account.

None of your data is sent to external news services.

### Device permissions

We ask for photo library access only when you choose to upload a cover image. We don't request camera or location access.

### How long we keep your data

We keep your profile and articles for as long as your account exists. If you delete an article, we delete its image first, then the record — so no orphaned image keeps costing storage on its own.

### How to delete your account and data

Email us at **privacy@trikedevs.com** to request account deletion. We'll remove your profile, articles, and cover images. Articles already seen by other people may still exist in copies outside our control (for example, screenshots), the same as any content published on the internet.

### Children

News App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under that age.

### Changes to this policy

If we change something material, we update the date at the top of this document and, if the change affects how we use your data, we notify you inside the app.

### Contact

Any question about this policy: **privacy@trikedevs.com**.
